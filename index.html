First-person puzzle

Portal & Portal 2

Two portals, one gun, and physics puzzles that teach you their rules without a single tutorial screen. GLaDOS and Wheatley carry some of the funniest writing in games. Portal is a tight 3-hour classic; Portal 2 adds a full story campaign plus a separate two-player co-op campaign.

Single-player2-player co-op (Portal 2)
Roguelike deckbuilder

Slay the Spire 2

The sequel to the game that defined the genre. Build a deck card by card as you climb, combine relics into absurd synergies, and lose a run because of one greedy pick. Short runs, endless replay value, and now with co-op climbs.

Single-playerOnline co-op
Underwater survival & exploration

Subnautica

You crash on an ocean planet and the only way forward is down. Beautiful reefs turn into pitch-black trenches with things moving in them. The story is found, not told, and every new depth you can reach feels earned. One of the best survival games ever made.

Single-player
Factory automation

Factorio

You start mining iron by hand and end up running a rail-fed megabase that launches rockets. Every bottleneck is a puzzle you created yourself. Dangerously addictive, superbly optimized, and the Space Age expansion takes it to other planets.

Single-playerOnline co-op
3D factory building

Satisfactory

Factorio-style production chains, but first-person in a huge hand-crafted alien world. You build up, across canyons and through jungles, with conveyor spaghetti you can walk under. Great for friends who like building more than fighting.

Single-playerCo-op up to 4
Vampire survival action RPG

V Rising

Survival crafting with real action-RPG combat. Raise a castle, hunt V Blood bosses for new powers, and plan every trip around the sunlight that burns you. Play it solo, with friends on PvE, or on PvP servers.

Single-playerCo-op / PvP servers
Viking survival & co-op building

Valheim

A Norse afterlife where each boss unlocks the next biome. The building system is satisfying, sailing in a storm is genuinely tense, and it is at its best as a group project with up to 10 friends on one world.

Single-playerCo-op up to 10
Real-time strategy

Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition

The RTS that people still play competitively more than 25 years after release. Dozens of civilizations, historical campaigns from Joan of Arc to Genghis Khan, and a remaster that keeps the original feel while fixing the old pathing pain.

CampaignsOnline multiplayer
Turn-based CRPG (D&D 5e)

Baldur's Gate 3

A full Dungeons & Dragons campaign where your choices change what happens, sometimes hours later. Combat is a systemic sandbox: shove, burn, stack crates, talk your way out. Excellent companions, and the whole campaign can be played in co-op.

Single-playerCo-op up to 4
Precision platformer

Celeste

Climb a mountain one screen at a time with some of the tightest controls in any platformer. It is hard, but checkpoints are instant and an Assist Mode is there if you need it. The story about anxiety and the soundtrack both hit harder than you would expect.

Single-player
Turn-based RPG with real-time parries

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33

Classic turn-based combat where you dodge and parry enemy attacks in real time, so every fight keeps you engaged. A Belle Époque–inspired world, a story with real emotional weight, and a standout soundtrack. One of the most acclaimed RPGs of recent years.

Single-player
Top-down mining sandbox

Core Keeper

Dig outward from a mysterious core through an underground world of biomes, bosses, farms and automation. Think Terraria seen from above. It is relaxed when you want it to be and great with up to 8 friends.

Single-playerCo-op up to 8
Voxel survival action RPG

Enshrouded

A large open world you can dig through and build anywhere, block by block, while pushing back a deadly fog. Real class builds and dungeon exploration set it apart from pure crafting games, and the base building is among the best in the genre.

Single-playerCo-op up to 16
Action roguelike

Hades

Fight your way out of the Greek underworld with fast, precise combat and boons from the Olympian gods. Dying is part of the story: each run moves relationships and plot forward, so even failure feels like progress. Fully voiced and beautifully drawn.

Single-player
Metroidvania

Hollow Knight

A huge, hand-drawn ruined kingdom of bugs to explore, map and slowly understand. Combat is demanding but fair, the bosses are memorable, and the amount of content for the price is absurd. When you finish, Silksong is waiting.

Single-player
Story-driven third-person action

Mafia: Definitive Edition

A ground-up remake of the 2002 Czech classic from Illusion Softworks. Taxi driver Tommy Angelo rises through the family in 1930s Lost Heaven. It plays like a gangster film: cinematic, linear and focused, with period cars and tommy-gun shootouts.

Single-player
Creature-collecting survival

Palworld

Catch creatures called Pals, then put them to work in your base, ride them, or fight alongside them with guns. It is chaotic and silly in the best way, and the base automation gives it real depth. Best enjoyed with friends.

Single-playerOnline co-op
